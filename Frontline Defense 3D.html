FRONTLINE DEFENSE

Tactical Command System

BUILD YOUR LINE • HOLD THE FRONT • 12 WAVES

HOW TO PLAY

Defend the HQ from 12 waves of enemies. Place defense units on the grid, upgrade them up to Level 5, and exploit synergies.

Controls

  • Select a unit from the right panel, then click a highlighted grid cell to deploy.
  • Click a deployed tower to upgrade (max Level 5) or sell it.
  • Waves start automatically. After each wave a 10-second countdown appears — click to deploy early or wait for auto-start.

Units & Evolution

  • Machine Gun — Evolves from pistol → SMG → assault → heavy MG → minigun style.
  • Sniper — Starts basic, gains better scope, bipod, and targeting optics each level.
  • Cannon — Grows stronger and larger; explosive radius increases every level.
  • Missile — Becomes more modern and powerful; splash area grows with level.

Synergy System

Precision Link (MG + Sniper nearby)
Sniper fire rate +30% • Machine Gun range +20%
Heavy Battery (Cannon + Missile nearby)
Splash +35% and damage +18% for both
Suppressive Fire (2+ Machine Guns close)
All nearby MGs gain +25% fire rate
Overwatch (Sniper near any tower)
Nearby towers receive +12% damage
Cluster Barrage (2+ Cannons close)
Significant splash radius increase
Guided Network (Missile near Cannon/Missile)
Missile damage +20%
